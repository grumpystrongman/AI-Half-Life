# Conference Abstract Draft

## Working title

Beyond Accuracy: Measuring the Half-Life of Healthcare AI Value

## Abstract

Healthcare organizations are rapidly moving artificial intelligence from pilot projects into operational workflows. Predictive models, ambient documentation tools, clinical decision support systems, revenue cycle automation, and generative AI assistants are increasingly being deployed at scale.

Governance and monitoring programs have improved substantially. Organizations are learning to measure bias, safety, fairness, drift, model performance, and implementation risk. Yet a critical question remains underdeveloped:

How long does a deployed AI system continue to create meaningful value after launch?

This session introduces AI Half-Life, a proposed framework for measuring the time required for an AI-enabled intervention to lose 50 percent of its realized value. The framework distinguishes technical model deterioration from operational value deterioration. A model can remain statistically healthy while workflow adoption, trust, financial benefit, and governance sustainability decline.

The presentation will summarize a structured review of public AI deployment case studies and healthcare AI governance literature. The review suggests that public reporting is comparatively strong on launch activity, model monitoring, early adoption, and short-term workflow outcomes, but weaker on long-term value persistence, recalibration criteria, retirement decisions, and time-to-value-failure.

The session will propose a practical measurement model using value realization, adoption retention, workflow impact, financial retention, and governance sustainability. It will also discuss how survival analysis, reliability engineering, and statistical process control can be adapted to post-deployment AI governance.

Attendees will leave with a practical vocabulary for asking a question many organizations have not yet formalized: when is an AI system still valuable, when should it be recalibrated, and when should it be retired?

## Learning objectives

1. Distinguish model performance decay from realized value decay.
2. Understand the AI Half-Life concept as a post-deployment governance metric.
3. Identify practical signals of value decay, including adoption loss, workflow mismatch, financial erosion, and governance fatigue.
4. Apply a basic framework for deciding whether to sustain, recalibrate, redesign, or retire deployed AI systems.
